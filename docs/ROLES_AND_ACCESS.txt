PAYROLL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - ROLES, ACCESS AND RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================

This document describes what each role can see and do in every module, who is
responsible for each step, and the rules that keep payroll safe. It matches how
the system behaves today.

1. THE THREE ROLES AT A GLANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ADMINISTRATOR (HR / payroll owner)
  Owns the company data, policy, final approval and money. Creates accounts,
  sets salaries, approves payroll, records salary payments, and can act on any
  employee. The administrator is not on payroll and has no attendance.

MANAGER (line manager)
  A real employee on payroll who also runs a team. Each team member has exactly
  one manager ("Reports to"). The manager handles the team's day-to-day
  decisions: attendance corrections, leave, first-level view on loans and
  advances, and preparing the team's payroll. A manager also has the normal
  employee portal for their own attendance, leave, pay and requests.

EMPLOYEE
  Self-service for their own records only: attendance, leave, payslips, loans,
  advances, reports. An employee never sees anyone else's data.

Landing page after sign-in:
  Administrator -> Overview (/admin)
  Manager       -> My team (/employee/team)
  Employee      -> Home (/employee)


2. SIGNING IN AND ACCOUNTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Employees sign in with their email and password on the login page.
- Sessions are stored on the server and last 8 hours. Signing out ends the
  session on the server. Nothing is kept in the browser's storage.
- After 10 failed sign-in attempts for the same email and device within 15
  minutes, sign-in is paused for 15 minutes.
- Accounts are created by the administrator (Employees > Add employee). The
  administrator enters the email and a TEMPORARY password. The new user must
  choose their own password at first sign-in; until then nothing else works.
  The administrator can untick "Require the employee to set their own password"
  to hand over a permanent password instead.
- Forgot password: the login page tells the user to contact HR. The
  administrator sets a new temporary password in Employees > Edit, which also
  signs that person out on every device. (There is no self-service reset yet.)
- Any signed-in user can change their own password in Settings > Company &
  account (needs the current password; minimum 8 characters).
- A deactivated account loses access immediately.
- Signing in does NOT mark attendance. Employees and managers press
  "Check in" on their Home or Attendance page when the day starts and "Check out"
  when it ends (see Attendance below).


3. HOW REQUESTS ARE ROUTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every approval follows the same rule so that nothing is stuck and nobody
approves their own request:

  a) If the employee has a manager, the request goes to that manager first.
  b) If the employee has no manager, it goes straight to the administrator.
  c) A manager's own requests go to the manager they report to, or to the
     administrator if they have none. A manager can never approve their own.
  d) ESCALATION: a request still undecided after 3 days (setting:
     APPROVAL_ESCALATION_DAYS) is flagged "escalated" and also appears in the
     administrator's queue, who may then decide it.
  e) The administrator can always decide leave and attendance requests (an
     override); loans and advances follow the recommendation rule in section 4.

Employees can always see who their pending request is "With".

Live badges: the menu shows a count next to each item that has work waiting for
the signed-in person. Counts refresh about every 30 seconds while the tab is
open, when you return to the tab, and straight after you act. The administrator
only sees what is theirs to act on (requests with no manager, escalated
requests, recommended loans/advances, payroll awaiting approval).

5. THE MONTHLY PAYROLL PROCESS, STEP BY STEP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1  Prepare the data (Manager for the team, Administrator for the rest)
        Resolve pending leave, correct attendance, check the month's checklist.
        Payroll uses the attendance and approved leave on record.

Step 2  CALCULATE (Manager: Team payroll > Calculate payroll)
        Draft payslips are created using: salary effective on each day
        (versions), calendar-day proration by joining and leaving dates,
        unpaid leave, late-sign-in deduction, loan and advance recoveries,
        EPF and professional tax. Deductions over 50% of earnings raise a
        warning; a negative take-home amount is blocked. The manager can
        recalculate a draft as often as needed.

Step 3  SUBMIT (Manager)
        Drafts are sent to the administrator. Blocked while the employee still
        has pending leave for the month. Changing a submitted payslip returns it
        to draft and it must be submitted again.

Step 4  APPROVE (Administrator: Payroll > Manage payroll)
        The administrator checks the figures and approves ("Approve and lock
        payroll"), or sends it back with a reason. Approval re-checks that
        salary, attendance and employment data have not changed, and that no
        leave is pending. An approved payslip is locked.

Step 5  PUBLISH (Manager, or Administrator for unmanaged employees)
        Only now can the employee see the payslip, its PDF and the figures in
        their report. Reopening an approved payslip withdraws it from the
        employee.

Step 6  PAY (Administrator)
        After paying through the bank, the administrator records the payment
        date and reference. Reversing a payment record needs a reason.

Every step is written to an audit history: who did it, when, and why.


6. ROLE GUIDES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 ADMINISTRATOR - responsibilities
  People
    * Create accounts (email + temporary password), set role and "Reports to".
    * Keep salary structures correct; every change needs an effective date and
      a reason and is kept as history.
    * Reset passwords for people who are locked out.
    * Deactivate leavers.
  Day to day
    * Work the Approvals inbox: requests for employees with no manager,
      escalated requests, recommended loans and advances.
    * Fix attendance in any month (reopen payroll first if it is finalized).
  Money
    * Approve loans and advances and record the payment; pause or resume
      recovery when needed.
    * Approve payroll, record salary payments, publish where there is no
      manager, reopen payroll for corrections with a reason.
  Oversight
    * Company reports, employee reports and exports.

6.2 MANAGER - responsibilities
  Own work (as an employee)
    * Own attendance, leave, payslips, loans, advances and reports.
    * Own requests are decided by the manager they report to, else the
      administrator.
  Team work (direct reports only)
    * Keep the team's attendance accurate: approve or reject correction
      requests, mark attendance, check "not marked yet" on My team.
    * Decide team leave promptly (a reason is needed to reject). Requests left
      undecided for 3 days escalate to the administrator.
    * Give a first-level view on team loans and advances: recommend or decline
      with a note. The administrator makes the final decision.
    * Prepare team payroll: calculate, review, submit for approval, then
      publish after the administrator approves.
    * Use the Team report for monthly attendance and leave (no salary data).
  Limits
    * Cannot approve or pay payroll, create or edit employees, see other teams,
      download team payslip PDFs, or change a month whose payroll is finalized.

6.3 EMPLOYEE - responsibilities
    * Check in when the day starts and check out when it ends. If a day or a
      time is wrong or missed (for example a forgotten check-out), request a
      correction with a reason, and the time, within 60 days.
    * Apply for leave in advance, attach medical documents where relevant, and
      follow the status (and who it is with).
    * Request loans or advances when needed; follow the decision, payment and
      recovery in the same screens.
    * View published payslips, download the PDF, and check the monthly report.
    * Keep their password private and change it from Settings.
  Limits
    * Sees only their own data. Cannot see other employees, salary structures,
      draft or unpublished payslips, or any approval screens.


7. RULES AND CONTROLS THAT APPLY TO EVERYONE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Separation of duties
  * Nobody approves their own request. A manager prepares and publishes team
    payroll but only the administrator approves and pays it.
  * Managers cannot change attendance, or approve leave, for a month whose
    payroll is finalized.

Data isolation
  * Managers see only their direct reports. Anything outside the team behaves as
    if it does not exist.
  * Employees see only their own records, enforced on the server, not just on
    screen.
  * Admin-only functions return "forbidden" to everyone else.

Audit trail
  * Leave decisions: who and when.
  * Attendance: who marked or changed each day, and when.
  * Loans and advances: every action, including manager recommendations, with
    the person and time.
  * Payroll: generated, updated, submitted, reviewed, approved, published,
    reopened, payment recorded or reversed - each with person, time and reason
    and the values before and after.
  * Salary changes: effective date, reason and who entered them.

Security
  * Passwords are stored hashed. Temporary passwords must be replaced at first
    sign-in. Sessions are server-side and end on sign-out, expiry, deactivation,
    or password reset.
  * Sign-in attempts are rate limited.


8. WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES NOT DO (YET)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * Self-service password reset by email (today: ask the administrator).
  * Email or SMS notifications. Alerts are the live badges inside the app, and
    only managers and administrators have them.
  * A manager's delegate (acting manager) while they are on leave. Escalation to
    the administrator after 3 days covers the gap.
  * Salary-revision requests from managers.
  * Custom roles or editable permissions (the three roles are fixed).
  * Holiday calendars and shift patterns. The payroll checklist treats Monday to
    Friday as working days as an advisory baseline only.
